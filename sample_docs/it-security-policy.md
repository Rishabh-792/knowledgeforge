# Larkspur Dynamics — IT Security Policy (v3.2)

_Fictional sample document for KnowledgeForge demos. Larkspur Dynamics is an
invented company; any resemblance to a real organization is coincidental._

## 1. Passwords and Authentication

All passwords must be at least fourteen characters long and generated by the
approved password manager. Password rotation is required every ninety days for
privileged accounts and every one hundred eighty days for standard accounts.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every system that supports it,
with hardware keys required for production infrastructure access.

Account lockouts can be resolved by the service desk at
helpdesk@larkspur.example or extension 4-1200.

## 2. Remote Access

Remote work requires the corporate VPN client. Employees must connect to the
VPN before opening any internal tool, including the wiki, the ticketing
system, and source control. VPN sessions expire after twelve hours and require
multi-factor authentication to renew. Split tunneling is disabled by policy.

## 3. Device Management

Company laptops are enrolled in mobile device management on first boot. Full
disk encryption is enforced and cannot be disabled by the user. Personal
devices may access email and calendar only through the managed mobile apps and
never through native mail clients.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported within four hours to the security
operations team so a remote wipe can be issued.

## 4. Data Classification

Information is classified into three tiers:

| Tier | Label | Examples | Handling |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Public | Marketing pages, published docs | No restrictions |
| 2 | Internal | Wikis, runbooks, org charts | Employees and contractors under NDA |
| 3 | Restricted | Customer data, credentials, financials | Named individuals, encrypted at rest and in transit |

Restricted data may never be pasted into external tools, including public
AI assistants, translation sites, or personal cloud storage.

## 5. Incident Reporting

Suspected phishing, malware, or data exposure must be reported within one hour
of discovery through the security portal. Do not attempt to investigate on
your own; preserving evidence matters more than speed of cleanup. The security
operations team runs a blameless post-incident review for every confirmed
incident within five business days.

## 6. Access Reviews

Access reviews run every quarter. Managers confirm that each direct report
still needs every entitlement they hold. Unconfirmed access is revoked
automatically after fourteen days. Exceptions require a written risk
acceptance signed by the information security officer.

# Larkspur Dynamics — Expense and Travel Policy (v2.4)

_Fictional sample document for KnowledgeForge demos. Larkspur Dynamics is an
invented company; all figures are illustrative._

## 1. Scope

This policy covers business travel, client entertainment, and all corporate
card spending. It applies to employees and to contractors whose statement of
work permits reimbursable expenses.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

| Trip or purchase value | Approver |
|------|------|
| Up to 2,500 dollars | Direct manager |
| 2,501 to 10,000 dollars | Department director |
| 10,001 to 40,000 dollars | Divisional vice president |
| Above 40,000 dollars | Chief financial officer |

Travel to a client site must be approved before booking, never after. Any trip
booked without prior approval is reimbursed at the discretion of the finance
controller.

## 3. Per-Diem Allowances

Meals and incidentals are paid as a flat per-diem rather than by receipt.
Domestic travel is paid at sixty-eight dollars per day. International travel is
paid at ninety-four dollars per day. Travel days at the start and end of a trip
are paid at seventy-five percent of the applicable rate.

Per-diem is not paid for any day on which two or more meals are provided by a
conference or a host client.

## 4. Lodging and Transport

Hotel spend is capped at two hundred forty dollars per night in designated
tier-one metros and one hundred sixty-five dollars per night everywhere else.
Rail is the required mode for journeys under three hundred miles where a direct
service exists.

Air travel is booked in economy for scheduled flight times under six hours.
Premium economy is permitted above six hours. Business class requires written
vice-president approval and is only available on segments longer than
eleven hours. Personal mileage is reimbursed at fifty-eight cents per mile.

## 5. Corporate Cards

The default corporate card limit is seven thousand five hundred dollars per
month. Cards are strictly for business use; personal charges must be repaid to
Accounts Payable within five business days of the statement date.

A card is automatically suspended after two consecutive statement cycles with
unreconciled transactions. Reinstatement requires manager sign-off and a short
refresher module in the finance portal.

## 6. Submission and Reimbursement

Expense reports must be submitted within forty-five calendar days of the trip
end date. Reports filed after that window require a written exception from the
finance controller and may be treated as taxable income.

Approved reports are settled in the disbursement run held every second Tuesday.

## 7. Entertainment

Client entertainment is capped at one hundred twenty dollars per attendee.
Gifts to any single client contact may not exceed one hundred dollars in a
calendar year, and gifts to public officials are prohibited outright.

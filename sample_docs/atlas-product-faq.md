# Atlas Routing Platform — Product FAQ

_Fictional sample document for KnowledgeForge demos. Atlas is an invented
logistics SaaS product by the invented company Larkspur Dynamics._

## What is Atlas?

Atlas is Larkspur Dynamics' route-optimization platform for mid-size delivery
fleets. It plans multi-stop routes, re-plans them live as traffic and order
volume change, and feeds driver apps with turn-by-turn instructions.

## How is Atlas priced?

Atlas is priced per active vehicle per month across three plans. The Starter
plan covers up to twenty-five vehicles with daily route planning. The Growth
plan adds live re-planning and the driver mobile app. The Enterprise plan adds
the optimization API, custom SLAs, and single sign-on.

## Does Atlas integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. Atlas ships connectors for common order-management and warehouse systems,
a webhook stream for route events, and a REST API for everything else. Most
customers complete their first integration within two weeks.

## What service levels do you offer?

The Enterprise plan carries a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment with
service credits. Support response targets are one hour for critical issues,
four hours for high, and one business day for normal requests. Status is
published on a public status page with incident history.

## How does Atlas handle our data?

Customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Fleet telemetry is retained
for thirteen months for analytics, then aggregated and anonymized. Customers
can export all of their data at any time and request deletion within thirty
days of contract end.

## Can drivers use their own phones?

Yes. The driver app supports recent versions of the two major mobile
platforms, works offline for pre-planned routes, and syncs automatically when
coverage returns. Fleet-supplied devices can be locked to kiosk mode.

## How long does onboarding take?

A typical Growth-plan fleet is live in three weeks: week one for data import
and depot setup, week two for dispatcher training and a pilot route group, and
week three for full-fleet rollout with a success engineer on call.

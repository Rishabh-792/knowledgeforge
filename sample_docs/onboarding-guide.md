# Larkspur Dynamics — New Hire Onboarding Guide

_Fictional sample document for KnowledgeForge demos._

Welcome to Larkspur Dynamics! This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Day One

Your laptop and badge are ready at the facilities desk on floor two. Sign in
with the temporary credentials sent to your personal email and complete the
enrollment wizard; it installs the VPN client, the password manager, and the
endpoint protection agent automatically.

Book the mandatory security-awareness training before the end of your first
week — the invite is already on your calendar.

## Your First Week

- Meet your onboarding buddy; they are your first stop for any question.
- Read the IT Security Policy and acknowledge it in the compliance portal.
- Join your team channels: announcements, your squad channel, and #ask-it.
- Set up your development environment using the platform team's bootstrap
  script; it takes roughly forty minutes on a fresh laptop.

## Payroll and Benefits

Payroll runs on the last business day of each month. Submit your bank details
in the HR portal by the fifteenth to be included in the current cycle. Health
coverage starts on your first day; dependents can be added within thirty days
of your start date without a qualifying event.

Expense reports are submitted through the finance portal. Receipts are
mandatory for any purchase above twenty-five dollars, and reimbursement lands
within two pay cycles.

## Time Off

Everyone accrues twenty-two vacation days per year, plus regional public
holidays. Request time off in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for
absences longer than three days. Sick days do not require advance approval —
just notify your manager before your usual start time.

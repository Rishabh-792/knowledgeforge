# Larkspur Dynamics — Benefits and Leave Guide

_Fictional sample document for KnowledgeForge demos. Larkspur Dynamics is an
invented company; all plan names and figures are invented._

## 1. Paid Time Off

Vacation accrues monthly rather than in a lump sum at the start of the year.
Unused vacation carries over to the following year up to a maximum of five
days, and carried-over days expire on 31 March if not taken.

Employees may borrow up to three days of vacation ahead of accrual with manager
approval. Balances are paid out at separation at the final base salary rate.

## 2. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive eight weeks. Leave may be taken in up to three
separate blocks but must be completed within twelve months of the birth or
placement.

There is no minimum service requirement — parental leave is available from the
first day of employment. Returning parents may work a reduced four-day schedule
at full pay for their first month back.

## 3. Sabbatical

Sabbatical eligibility begins after seven years of continuous service. Eligible
employees may take four consecutive weeks of paid sabbatical in addition to
their vacation balance, and become eligible again every five years thereafter.

Sabbatical requests must be filed six months in advance so the team can plan
cover. Sabbatical time cannot be split into shorter periods or exchanged for
pay.

## 4. Health Plan Tiers

| Tier | Plan type | Annual deductible | Employer premium share |
|------|------|------|------|
| Basin | High-deductible with health savings account | 3,200 dollars | Eighty percent |
| Ridge | Standard preferred-provider plan | 1,400 dollars | Eighty-five percent |
| Summit | Low-deductible plan with expanded dental and vision | 500 dollars | Ninety percent |

Employees on the Basin tier receive an employer health-savings contribution of
one thousand two hundred dollars per year for individual coverage and two
thousand four hundred dollars for family coverage.

## 5. Enrollment Windows

Open enrollment runs from 1 November to 21 November each year, with elections
effective the following 1 January. Outside that window, changes require a
qualifying life event — marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage —
reported within forty-five days of the event.

## 6. Other Benefits

- Wellness stipend of seventy-five dollars per month, claimable quarterly.
- Learning budget of two thousand dollars per employee per calendar year,
  usable for courses, conferences, and books.
- Commuter allowance of ninety dollars per month for public transit.
- Employee assistance programme with eight counselling sessions per year at no
  cost.

# Larkspur Dynamics — Procurement and Vendor Policy (v1.9)

_Fictional sample document for KnowledgeForge demos. Larkspur Dynamics is an
invented company; all vendors referenced are hypothetical._

## 1. Purchase Thresholds

| Annual contract value | Requirement |
|------|------|
| Under 1,000 dollars | Manager approval only, no quotes needed |
| 1,000 to 15,000 dollars | One written quote plus procurement review |
| 15,001 to 75,000 dollars | Three competitive quotes |
| Above 75,000 dollars | Formal request for proposal and steering committee sign-off |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to stay under a threshold is a policy
violation and is caught by the quarterly spend audit.

## 2. Vendor Onboarding

Before a first payment can be released, a vendor must supply tax documentation,
complete banking verification by callback to a publicly listed number, and sign
the Larkspur master services agreement. Onboarding is targeted to complete
within ten business days of a complete submission.

Every approved vendor is added to the vendor register with an owner of record.
A vendor with no owner is placed on hold at the next quarterly review.

## 3. Vendor Tiering

- **Tier 1** — processes Restricted data or supports revenue-generating systems.
  Reassessed every twelve months.
- **Tier 2** — internal tooling without Restricted data. Reassessed every two
  years.
- **Tier 3** — low-risk goods and one-off services. No periodic reassessment.

## 4. Security Review

A security review is mandatory for any vendor that will process Restricted data
and for any software purchase above ten thousand dollars per year, regardless of
data class.

Vendors must provide either a SOC 2 Type II report issued within the previous
twelve months or complete the ninety-six-question Larkspur vendor security
questionnaire. Standard review turnaround is ten business days; an expedited
review can be requested for five business days with divisional vice-president
sponsorship.

Findings rated high must be remediated or formally risk-accepted before signing.
Critical findings block the contract outright with no exception path.

## 5. Contract Terms

Standard payment terms are net forty-five days. Automatic renewal clauses longer
than twelve months are not accepted. Every agreement must include termination
for convenience with sixty days' written notice.

Liability caps below two times the annual contract value require legal review.
Vendors handling Restricted data must accept the Larkspur data processing
addendum, including breach notification to Larkspur within twenty-four hours.

## 6. Prohibited Practices

Employees may not sign vendor agreements without delegated signature authority,
accept gifts that could influence a selection decision, or begin work under a
verbal commitment before a purchase order exists.

Contact procurement@larkspur.example or extension 4-5000 for sourcing support,
and vendor-security@larkspur.example for review status.
